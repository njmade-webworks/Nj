Case Study

The Golden Birthday

Creating an elegant, animated birthday card that feels luxurious and personal.

The Goal

Birthday cards often feel generic. I wanted to create a template that felt premium and memorable, making the recipient feel truly special.

What I Did

I focused on elegant typography, smooth fade-in animations, and a soft, warm color palette. I built this using beginner-friendly web tools, proving you do not need complex code to make something beautiful.

  • Designed a clean, mobile-first layout.
  • Added subtle scroll animations for a premium feel.
  • Created a simple system for clients to customize names and dates.

The Result

This became one of my most requested designs. Clients love how easy it is to share via text message, and recipients always comment on how beautiful and thoughtful it is.

Case Study

Holiday Magic Series

Interactive seasonal cards that bring holiday spirit to life.

The Goal

To create distinct, festive experiences for different holidays while maintaining a clean, professional design standard.

What I Did

I designed separate visual themes for Christmas and Valentine's Day. For Christmas, I used cool blues and whites with subtle "snowfall" animations. For Valentine's, I used warm pinks and soft heart transitions.

The Result

These templates are perfect for clients who want something festive but still elegant. They are quick to customize and always bring a smile to the recipient's face.
