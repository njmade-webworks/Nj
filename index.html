Golden Birthday Collection

Creating elegant, animated birthday cards that feel luxurious and personal.

The Challenge

Birthday cards often feel generic and impersonal. The goal was to create a collection that felt premium, personalized, and memorable—something that would make the recipient feel truly special on their day.

The Approach

I focused on three key elements: elegant typography, smooth animations, and customizable color palettes. Each card features gold accents, subtle particle effects, and carefully timed transitions that create a sense of occasion.

Features

  • • Smooth scroll-triggered animations
  • • Customizable color schemes
  • • Photo upload integration
  • • Background music options

Results

  • • 50+ cards sold in first month
  • • 98% client satisfaction
  • • Average 5-star rating
  • • 40% repeat customers

The Outcome

The Golden Birthday Collection became one of the most popular offerings, with clients praising the attention to detail and the emotional impact of the animations. Many reported that recipients were moved to tears by the thoughtful design.

Holiday Magic Series

Interactive seasonal cards that bring holiday spirit to life.

The Challenge

Holiday cards need to capture the unique spirit of each season while maintaining a cohesive brand identity. The challenge was creating distinct experiences for Christmas, Halloween, and Valentine's Day that all felt authentically "Nj Made."

The Solution

Each holiday received its own visual language and interaction patterns. Christmas cards feature snow animations and warm color palettes. Halloween cards use playful spooky elements with interactive surprises. Valentine's cards focus on romantic animations and heartfelt messaging.

Key Features

Christmas

Falling snow, gift animations, and festive music integration

Halloween

Interactive elements, surprise reveals, and playful animations

Valentine's

Floating hearts, romantic transitions, and love letter effects
